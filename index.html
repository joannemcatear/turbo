Help & tips
  • Spacing your rides. Do the three sessions in order with a day between them, e.g. Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday. Keep the hard rides (the 1st and 3rd of each week) away from the day after a hard run; easy runs fit anywhere.
  • Too hard or too easy? During a workout, the clicker's increase/decrease buttons change the intensity by 5% (big steps by 10%). If you're regularly changing it, update your FTP.
  • ERG feels like a wall? ERG holds the power whatever your cadence, so if you slow down it gets harder. Keep your cadence up (85–95 rpm) and use an easy gear.
  • Close Zwift first. The Hub One only accepts one Bluetooth connection, so Zwift / Zwift Companion must be fully closed.
  • Click v2 is two devices. Press Add clicker once for each half (left and right). Press a button on the puck to wake it before connecting.
  • Click v2 dropping out every minute? Zwift locks the Click v2 to its own app. Open Zwift, pair the trainer and Click v2 for ~30 seconds, close Zwift completely, then connect here. This "unlock" lasts about a day. Until then, this app reconnects automatically. The right half usually works without unlocking, so + and B are set up to cover both directions.
  • Buttons not reacting? The Click v2 may hide its buttons until its firmware is up to date — update it in the Zwift Companion app.
